ABOUT LUMIÈRE

Founded in 2023 by Shimon Elbaz, Lumière was born out of a passion for unmatched brilliance, craftsmanship, and timeless elegance. We have quickly established ourselves as a reliable and dependable cornerstone in the jewelry industry, celebrated for delivering both all-time classic masterpieces and cutting-edge trending designs.
